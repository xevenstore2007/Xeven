1. Our 14-day, no-questions-asked promise

If you are not satisfied with your XEVEN purchase, we will refund you within 14 days of your purchase date—no questions asked. We want you to feel comfortable trying our digital resources. You do not need to explain why you are requesting a refund.

2. How to request a refund

Email support@xeven.store within 14 calendar days of your purchase and write that you would like a refund. To help us locate the order, include the email address used at checkout and your order or receipt number if available.

  • Send your request within 14 days of the original purchase date.
  • We may use your order details only to identify the purchase and process the request.
  • We will confirm the request and arrange the refund through the checkout/payment process where possible.

3. Refund processing time

Once your request is approved, we will initiate the refund as promptly as reasonably possible. The time it takes for the funds to appear depends on the payment provider, bank, card issuer, and payment method. XEVEN cannot control those providers’ processing times.

4. Access after a refund

After a refund is issued, your license to use the refunded digital product ends. Please stop using the refunded materials and delete local copies of the original product files, except where retaining a copy is required by law.

5. Requests after 14 days

Our no-questions-asked refund promise applies to requests made within 14 calendar days of purchase. If something has gone wrong with delivery or access after that period, contact us anyway and we will review the issue fairly. Any rights you have under applicable consumer law remain unaffected.

6. Purchases through Payhip

If you purchased through Payhip, we may direct you to the order confirmation or checkout provider to help identify the transaction or complete the refund. Please also check the refund information presented during checkout. This policy describes XEVEN’s 14-day refund promise for XEVEN products.

7. Contact us

For a refund request or help with a purchase, contact support@xeven.store. We are happy to help.

Our promise: Not satisfied? Email us within 14 days of purchase and we will refund you—no questions asked. Mandatory rights under applicable law are not affected.
