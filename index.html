Main ebook · Chapter 2

The product validation scorecard

Score each factor from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong) using the evidence you collected, not hope.

  • 1 Customer demand
  • 2 Audience clarity
  • 3 Differentiation
  • 4 Supplier reliability
  • 5 Fulfilment feasibility
  • 6 Gross margin
  • 7 Marketing potential
  • 8 Legal, safety and IP risk

Total out of 40: 32–40 test it · 24–31 improve the weak factors · under 24 reconsider. The book walks through a full worked example.

Bonus 03 · 100+ AI Prompts

Prompt 2.01 · Benefit-bullet description

Every prompt comes with variables, a filled example and a pro tip.

Write a Shopify product description for [product] aimed at [buyer]. Real specs and facts: [specs]. Structure: one-line hook, 3 benefit bullets (benefit first, feature second), a short specs list, one closing line. Do not invent features, materials or certifications. Under 150 words.

Filled example in the book: a ceramic 12 oz coffee mug for busy people who drink coffee at their desk. Prompts cover product pages, SEO, ads, emails, support replies and more.
