1. Information we collect

XEVEN aims to collect only information reasonably needed to operate our website, provide digital products, respond to enquiries, and support purchases. The information we receive depends on how you interact with us.

  • Information you provide: such as your name, email address, and the contents of messages you send to support@xeven.store.
  • Purchase information: details related to an order may be processed by the checkout and payment services used for the purchase. We may receive limited order details needed to confirm or support your purchase.
  • Technical information: when you visit a website, hosting or security services may process information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages requested, and access times as part of delivering and protecting the site.

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary to resolve your enquiry.

2. How we use information

Depending on the information and your interaction with us, XEVEN may use it to:

  • respond to customer questions and provide support;
  • help confirm, deliver, or troubleshoot a digital-product purchase;
  • maintain, secure, and improve our website and customer experience;
  • prevent misuse, investigate suspected fraud, and protect our rights; and
  • meet applicable legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations.

We do not intend to sell personal information. We only use information for purposes compatible with the context in which it was provided, subject to applicable law.

3. Checkout and payments

Our website may send you to Payhip or another third-party checkout provider to place an order. Checkout, payment processing, order emails, and digital-file delivery may be handled by Payhip and the payment provider you choose. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms and practices.

XEVEN does not ask you to enter payment-card details directly into this website. Please review the relevant provider’s privacy policy before completing a purchase. You can read Payhip’s Privacy and Cookies Policy.

4. Sharing and service providers

We may rely on third-party services to host the website, process checkout and payments, deliver digital products, protect site security, or communicate with customers. These providers may process relevant information as needed to perform their services and under their own terms.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, or protect XEVEN, our customers, or others. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites and services.

5. Cookies, embedded media, and analytics

The XEVEN website may use essential technical features, and its hosting provider or third-party tools may use logs, cookies, or similar technologies. If we add analytics, advertising tools, or embedded content such as a video hosted by another platform, that service may collect technical or usage information when you view or interact with it.

Third-party cookies and tracking technologies are governed by the relevant provider. You can usually manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect how some external features work. If our use of these technologies changes materially, we will update this policy and add any consent controls required by applicable law.

6. Data retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to enquiries, supporting transactions, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the service provider involved.

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the website and information we handle to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no website, internet transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict some processing. These rights are subject to applicable law and may have exceptions.

To make a privacy request, contact support@xeven.store and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where required or permitted by law. If you receive optional marketing emails from us, you can use the unsubscribe method provided in the message. Transactional or support messages may still be sent when necessary.

8. Children’s privacy

Our website and digital products are intended for a general audience and are not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the services under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our website, services, or legal obligations. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
